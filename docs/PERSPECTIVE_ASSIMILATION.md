# Perspective Assimilation Discipline

Use this pattern when a system needs to learn from criticism, positive examples, and workflow
instruction without collapsing them into one confirmation-biased corpus.

## Three labeled lanes

1. **Adversarial perspectives** — steelman skeptical claims, split empirical claims from forecasts,
   values, aesthetics, labor, legal, environmental, and rhetorical claims, then assign evidence and
   falsifiable tests.
2. **Creative proof** — preserve transcript plus representative frames, extract visual and narrative
   DNA, and distinguish demonstrated output from vendor claims.
3. **Workflow upgrades** — extract techniques as candidates with prerequisites, risks, objective
   acceptance tests, and one closure state: `integrated`, `superseded`,
   `rejected_with_evidence`, `deferred_with_gate`, or `candidate_needs_verification`.

Do not merge the raw lanes. Synthesis may compare them only after each source is independently
classified and receipts exist.

## VERITY application

- Search before concluding; a supplied source is evidence intake, not truth.
- Steelman before refuting. A criticism can remain valid even when mitigation exists.
- Never invent an existing control. Mark system state unknown until locally verified.
- Separate the critic from the maker. The generator may not grade its own final output.
- Discovery is not integration. Close every candidate through a bounded proof against the original
  gap.
- Countermeasures improve engineering, governance, consent, provenance, disclosure, labor practice,
  research, or creative craft. They are never propaganda or suppression.
- Preserve human approval gates for payment, publishing, account/security policy, and destructive
  actions.

## Minimal validation contract

The run fails unless every supplied unique source is accounted for; transcripts exist; visual lanes
have frame evidence; analyses use the expected schema; adversarial claims have a steelman,
calibrated classification, mitigation state, and falsifiable test; workflow candidates have an
objective test and closure state; and the three corpora remain separately labeled.

This discipline is model- and platform-agnostic. The public harness should provide the gates; local
deployments may supply their own discovery, transcription, vision, storage, and scheduling adapters.
